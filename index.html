FAQ

Questions hospital owners ask

Is Health App right for a small clinic?

Yes. Clinics typically start with OPD appointments, tokens and billing, then add modules like pharmacy or HR & payroll as they grow. You only use what you need.

What does the free demo include?

A guided walkthrough of the modules you care about, on your own numbers and workflows — so you can judge it on your real operations, not a canned script.

How long does setup take?

Most small facilities are running within days: doctors, staff, departments and pricing rules are configured with you, then your team gets a short handover.

Do we need special hardware or IT staff?

No. If you have a phone or a computer with a browser, you can run Health App. No servers to maintain.

Will it work with our existing process?

Health App supports both online and walk-in patients in one common queue, so you can move to digital gradually instead of changing everything overnight.
