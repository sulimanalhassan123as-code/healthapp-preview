One platform, every department

Everything your hospital runs on

Each module talks to the others, so data entered once flows from registration to billing without re-typing.

🩺

OPD Management

Online appointments, digital tokens and live queue status with waiting-time estimates, doctor availability and future-date booking. One common queue for online and walk-in patients.

🏥

IPD Management

Admissions, floor/room/bed availability, doctor assignment, treatment and medicine charges, inpatient billing and discharge advice — fully paperless.

👥

EMS, HR & Payroll

Employee records, departments, attendance punch-in/out, working-hour tracking and rule-based payroll generation, integrated with Finance.

💊

Pharmacy & Inventory

Medicine records, stock management and billing integration — dispense against prescriptions without losing track of inventory.

🧾

Billing & Finance

OPD/IPD billing, medicine and service charges, final bill printouts, GST handling, expense and cash tracking, transaction records, payroll integration.

📅

Appointments & Booking

Online pre-booking around doctor availability, live token tracking and waiting-time visibility for patients and staff.
